## Probability

- Interpretations of Probability (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy): https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/probability-interpret/
- Bertrand paradox, three different answers to "what is the probability that a random chord of a circle is longer than the side of the inscribed equilateral triangle?" (Wikipedia): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand_paradox_(probability)

## Problem Solving & Brainteasers

- International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO), official archive of the competition problems of each year since 1959: https://www.imo-official.org/problems/
- El problema del mes (RSME), monthly problems of the Real Sociedad Matemática Española for different age categories, with solutions (in Spanish): https://www.rsme.es/category/el-problema-del-mes/
- Jane Street Puzzles, monthly puzzle with an archive of the past puzzles and their solutions: https://www.janestreet.com/puzzles/
- GRiddles (G-Research), puzzle competition with three series of four problems of increasing difficulty: https://www.gresearch.com/griddles/
- Project Euler, mathematical problems that need a computer program to solve: https://projecteuler.net/
- Brainstellar, puzzles from quant interviews sorted by difficulty (easy, medium, hard) and by topic (probability, discrete maths, strategy), with solutions: https://brainstellar.com/
- Hespcode, roadmap to prepare quant interviews for developers, researchers and traders (sign-in necessary): https://hespcode.com/
