# Interest Rates

Interest rate in a particular situation defines the amount of money a borrower promises to pay the lender. These include mortgage rates, deposit rates, ...

Interest rates are often expressed in basis points. One basis point is 0.01% per annum.

## Credit Risk

Credit risk is the risk that there will be a default by the borrower of funds, so that the interest and principal are not paid to the lender as promised. The higher the credit risk, the higher the interest rate that is promised by the borrower.

### Risk Free Rate

It is usually assumed that there is no chance that the government of a developed country will default on an obligation denominated in its own currency. For a developed country's Treasury bills or Treasury bonds, it is certain that interest and principal payments will be made as promised.

### Risk Spread

The extra amount added to a risk-free interest rate to allow for credit risk is known as credit spread.
