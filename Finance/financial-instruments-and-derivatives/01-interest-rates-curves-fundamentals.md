# Interest Rate Curves Fundamentals

## Structure of interest rates

The **term structure of interest rates** describes the relation between interest rates and their **tenors**. The tenor is the time to maturity of a fixed income security. Each security has its own interest rate, and this rate could depend on the length of the loan. For example, a longer loan usually pays a higher interest rate.

In the economy the interest rates could be measured in different ways.
